# Chromorrow Support

Chromorrow is a private visual life-planning app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my plan stored?

Your plan is stored on your device and, when iCloud is available, synchronized through your private iCloud database. Chromorrow does not require a separate account.

### How do I restore Chromorrow Plus?

Open **Settings → Chromorrow Plus → Restore Purchase** while signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

### How do I make a backup?

Open **Settings → Backup → Export Backup**. Keep the exported JSON file in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces the current synced plan.

### How do I delete my data?

Open **Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All My Data**. The deletion is also requested for devices synchronized through iCloud; an offline device may take time to reflect it.
